The Program

Designed for Steady, Real Growth

The Confident Learner Program is built for K–5 students who need support strengthening their math, reading, or writing skills, closing learning gaps, and becoming more independent learners.

This is more than weekly tutoring.

Your child receives a personalized academic plan, consistent instruction, progress monitoring, and ongoing support throughout the month — so growth doesn't stop between sessions.

Monthly Program Includes

Everything Your Child Needs to Keep Growing

A thoughtfully structured program with support that continues all month long.

📅
8 Private Instruction Sessions

Two personalized 60-minute sessions each week, designed around your child's specific needs, learning style, and academic goals.

🔍
Initial Academic Skill Review

We begin by identifying your child's current strengths, learning gaps, and priority areas for improvement.

📋
Personalized Learning Success Plan

A clear plan outlining the skills we will strengthen and the goals we will work toward together.

📬
Weekly Parent Updates

Regular updates explaining:

  • What we worked on
  • Progress your child is making
  • Areas that still need support
  • Ways you can reinforce learning at home

At-Home Practice Activities

Simple, targeted activities that support your child's progress without overwhelming your family.

📈
Progress Monitoring

Your child's growth is monitored throughout the program so instruction can be adjusted as their needs change.

Monthly Parent Progress Meeting

At the end of each month, we review your child's progress, celebrate improvements, and set goals for the following month.

💬
Between-Session Support

Reach out anytime with brief questions about assignments, learning concerns, or recommended practice.
